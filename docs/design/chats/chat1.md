I've put everything in **Routine App.dc.html** on one canvas you can pan around. The background check hasn't reported back yet.

It has all seven screens in light and dark, the four Today states (normal, shortened, wind down, before check-in), the block sheet over the timeline, and the lock-screen notifications. After those come the style sheet with hex values, type scale, spacing, radii and category tints, then notes on the three key transitions.

Things to know:
- **Font:** I used Manrope instead of Inter. It's a similar humanist sans with light weights and tabular figures. You can swap it back.
- **Accent colour:** sage is the default. You can switch to dusk blue in Tweaks, and every screen and the style sheet update together.
- **Errors aren't red:** the file-error card uses the warm food tint instead.
- **These are still mockups.** If you want to see the transitions moving, I can turn check-in, Today and the block sheet into a clickable prototype next.
