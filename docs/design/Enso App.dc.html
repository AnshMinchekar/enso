Enso · Android · v1 mockups

A quiet day, built from one input.

Every screen at 412 × 915 dp, morning, afternoon and evening side by side. Sample day: woke 06:05, Gym + Cook, one errand, lights out 22:30. Manrope stands in for Inter (humanist, light weights, tabular figures). Dusk blue is the only accent and only marks “now” and the primary action. Swap it for sage in Tweaks.

11

Key transitions

Springs, not curves. Nothing pops in.
Check-in → Today
  1. “Build my day” fades its label and morphs into the hero card’s shape (Hero widget, shared bounds).
  2. Picker and chips slide down 24 dp and fade out over 200 ms.
  3. Timeline rows stagger in from 12 dp below, 30 ms apart.
  4. The list settles scrolled to “now”. One soft confirmation haptic.
SpringDescription(mass 1, stiffness 260, damping 28) · ~380 ms
Card → sheet
  1. The tapped row or hero card grows into the sheet. Title and time are shared elements and keep their position.
  2. Corner radius goes 16 → 32. The scrim fades to full in the same spring.
  3. Detail rows and actions fade in once the sheet passes 70% height.
  4. Drag down to reverse at the same spring. Releasing fast dismisses.
stiffness 300, damping 30 · ~320 ms · velocity carried from the drag
Re-plan
  1. After +15 min, Running late or Skip, blocks after now slide to their new positions together (AnimatedList with implicit offsets).
  2. Changed times cross-fade digit by digit. Tabular figures keep the column from jumping.
  3. A skipped block fades and collapses its height. The next flexible block grows into the space.
  4. If something got shorter, the summary card eases in above the list. No colour change.
stiffness 220, damping 26 · ~400 ms · light tick haptic per tap
